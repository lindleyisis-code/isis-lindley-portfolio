About Me

Hello! I am a Texas State University Sophomore and Honors student pursuing a dual path in Business Management (HR Concentration) and Biology, with a minor in Psychology.

Standing at 80+ credit hours, I am a dedicated student leader, a frequent on the Dean's List, and deeply passionate about bridging healthcare with corporate leadership.

Leadership, empathy, and management aren't just skills I learned through school, as the oldest of four siblings, they have been part of my daily reality for as long as I can remember. My role model, my grandfather, worked in Human Resources and instilled in me the importance of advocating for employees within a company. With this, I started out yearning to create healthy work environments for everyday people, especially since my mother and grandmother work in the education system with 60+ hours a week. After some time in college, I've discovered another passion of mine; the human body. How the mind can create physiological change, how our biology reacts to our environment, and how we can use this intersection to measurably improve peoples daily life.

This conversation between health and business is ultimately where I see my future. I am committed to transforming how organizations care for the mind, body, and overall well-being of their people.

Thank you for taking the time to learn about me!
